# SH-105 — Sakhi

## AI-Powered Financial Empowerment for Rural Women and People with Limited Financial Literacy

---

# 1. Problem Statement

The core problem is not simply helping people manage money.

The larger problem is helping people **understand their financial situation and make better-informed financial decisions** despite limited financial literacy, complicated financial terminology, and limited access to understandable financial guidance.

For rural women and financially underserved users, financial information is often:

* Difficult to understand
* Filled with technical terminology
* Scattered across different sources
* Difficult to apply to their personal situation
* Hard to access in a familiar language

### Problem

> How can we make financial guidance simple, personalized, accessible, and actionable for people with limited financial literacy?

---

# 2. Solution — Sakhi

**Sakhi** is an AI-powered financial companion that helps users:

1. Understand their financial situation
2. Follow a personalized financial journey
3. Ask financial questions in simple language
4. Set and track savings goals
5. Learn important financial concepts
6. Discover relevant government schemes and benefits

The system combines:

**Financial calculations + personalized planning + AI explanations + government benefit discovery**

The AI does not independently calculate financial values.

Instead:

> **Backend calculates → AI explains → User understands**

# 4. Core Feature — Financial Journey

## Purpose

The Financial Journey answers:

> **"What should I focus on next?"**

It is inspired by the concept of financial milestones such as Ramsey's Baby Steps, but is adapted for the Indian and rural context.

Instead of blindly recommending investments, Sakhi determines the user's current financial stage and next milestone.

### Proposed Journey

```text
Track Income & Expenses
          ↓
Build Emergency Fund
          ↓
Manage / Clear Debt
          ↓
Build Savings
          ↓
Protect Family
          ↓
Long-Term Investing
          ↓
Financial Independence
```

The user can see:

* Current stage
* Completed stages
* Next milestone
* Progress toward the milestone
* Recommended actions

### Example

```text
Your Current Stage

💰 Manage Your Money

✓ Track monthly income
✓ Track monthly expenses

Next:

🛡 Build an Emergency Fund

Target: ₹30,000
Current: ₹12,000
Remaining: ₹18,000
```

The journey should provide **guidance rather than complex financial advice**.

# 7. AI Architecture

The AI must **not be responsible for financial calculations**.

### Architecture

```text
User
  ↓
React Frontend
  ↓
FastAPI Backend
  ↓
Financial Engine
  ↓
Structured Financial Data
  ↓
AI Service
  ↓
Simple Explanation
  ↓
User
```

### Example

User data:

```text
Income = ₹12,000
Expenses = ₹7,000
```

Backend:

```text
Surplus = ₹5,000
```

Goal:

```text
Target = ₹50,000
Current = ₹0
Time = 12 months

Required monthly saving ≈ ₹4,167
```

The AI receives the calculated values and explains them:

> "You have around ₹5,000 left after your monthly expenses. To reach your ₹50,000 goal in one year, you would need to save about ₹4,167 each month."

This keeps important calculations deterministic and auditable.

# 8. Goal Planner

### Example

```text

```

```text

Required monthly saving:
```

The system tracks progress toward the goal.

# 10. "Could This Help You?" Scheme Matching

Users answer simple questions.

Example:

```text

✓ Yes

Age requirement?
✓ Meets requirement

Income requirement?
✓ Meets requirement

Business requirement?
✓ Yes

State?
✓ Telangana
```

Sakhi then provides a preliminary result:

> **You may be eligible to explore this program.**

The application must clearly state:

> Sakhi provides a preliminary eligibility match and does not provide official eligibility confirmation.

# 11. Scheme Information

Each scheme should contain:

* Scheme name
* What it provides
* Who it is for
* Basic eligibility
* Required documents
* Application process
* Official source
* Relevant state
* Category

The official government source should always be shown so users can verify information and proceed with the actual application.

# 12. Financial Education — Learn with Sakhi

Financial concepts should be explained using simple language rather than textbook-style definitions.

### Initial Topics

* Budgeting
* Saving
* Emergency funds
* Loans
* Interest
* EMI
* Insurance
* SIPs / investing
* Government benefits

### Example

Instead of:

> "Compound interest is interest calculated on the initial principal and accumulated interest..."

Sakhi could explain:

> "If you regularly save ₹500 every month, your money can grow over time. Let's see how."

Education should be:

* Short
* Visual
* Conversational
* Practical
* Easy to understand

Interactive quizzes can be added if time permits, but is not necessary.

# 13. Loan Decoder

The Loan Decoder is a potential demo feature.

The user enters or uploads loan information.

### Example

```text
Loan Amount: ₹50,000
Interest: 18%
Tenure: 24 months
Fees: ₹2,000
```

Sakhi converts this into simple explanations:

* How much are you borrowing?
* How much will you repay?
* How much interest are you paying?
* What fees are involved?
* What does the interest rate mean?
* What is the approximate EMI?

The feature is secondary to the core MVP.

If development time becomes limited, it should be postponed.

# 15. UX Philosophy

Sakhi is **not** intended to look like a complicated financial dashboard.

The target audience may have limited financial literacy.

Therefore, the interface should prioritize:

* Large buttons
* Simple words
* Minimal forms
* Clear actions
* Large readable numbers
* Visual explanations
* Familiar icons
* Local language support
* Voice where possible
* Minimal financial jargon

### Design Principle

> **The user should understand what to do without needing to understand finance first.**

# 24. Important Technical Principle

Sakhi should follow a strict separation between **calculation** and **explanation**.

### Financial Engine

Responsible for:

* Income calculations
* Expense calculations
* Surplus
* Savings requirements
* Goal calculations
* Debt calculations
* EMI calculations
* Financial journey stage determination

### AI Service

Responsible for:

* Explaining calculations
* Simplifying financial concepts
* Answering general financial questions
* Converting structured results into conversational responses
* Helping users understand available options

### AI should NOT independently determine numerical financial results.

# 27. Final Product Philosophy

Sakhi is not trying to replace a bank, financial advisor, or government portal.

Its role is to act as a **financial understanding and navigation layer** between the user and the complicated financial system.

```text
Complex Financial World
          ↓
        SAKHI
          ↓
Simple Understanding
          ↓
Better-Informed Action
```
